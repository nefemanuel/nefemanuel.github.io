socialhousingR
======

An R package for accessing and harmonising official statistics on social housing and local authority housing finance in England. It brings the Local Authority Housing Statistics, revenue and capital outturn, the housing live tables and social housing sales into one consistent layout.

The package is still being developed. Function names and outputs may change, and it has not yet been released.
